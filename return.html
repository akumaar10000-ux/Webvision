Return and Exchange Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Return Eligibility

Return or exchange requests may be submitted within 2 days of receiving the product, subject to the applicable product terms and consumer protection laws. Products should generally be unused and in the same condition in which they were received, with their original packaging and accessories.

2. Products Not Eligible for Return

Certain product categories may be excluded from returns or exchanges. Any applicable exclusions should be clearly communicated before purchase. Sale items may have specific return conditions where permitted by applicable law.

3. Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, contact our support team within the applicable reporting period. Please provide your order details and photographs where possible so the issue can be reviewed.

4. Return and Exchange Process

Contact our support team before sending a product back. Once a return is received, the product may be inspected to determine whether it meets the applicable return conditions. We will inform you of the outcome and the next steps for an approved return or exchange.

5. Refunds

Refund eligibility and processing are governed by our Refund and Cancellation Policy and applicable law. Approved refunds are processed using the relevant payment method or another agreed method.

6. Contact Us

For return or exchange enquiries, contact us at info@dantanisgroup.online. Please include your order details and a description of the issue.
