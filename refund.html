Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

  1. Cancellation requests may be considered if submitted within 2 days of placing an order. However, cancellation may not be possible if the order has already been processed for shipping or dispatched by the seller or merchant.
  2. Cancellation requests for perishable items may not be accepted. If a delivered product is damaged, defective, or of unsatisfactory quality, please contact our support team to request a review for a possible refund or replacement, subject to the applicable product policy and law.
  3. Damaged or defective products should be reported within 2 days of delivery, along with relevant details and photographs where possible. Please retain the product and packaging until the issue has been reviewed.
  4. Once a refund is approved, the processing time will depend on the payment method, payment provider, and applicable banking procedures. We will communicate any relevant processing information when the refund is approved.
