Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Order Processing

Orders will be processed after the required order details and payment, where applicable, have been confirmed. Processing times may vary depending on product availability, payment confirmation, holidays, and other operational factors. Any applicable processing estimate will be communicated during the order process.

2. Shipping and Delivery

Where physical products are offered, orders may be shipped through available courier, postal, or other delivery services. Estimated dispatch and delivery timelines will depend on the destination, product availability, and the selected delivery service.

3. Delivery Address

Orders will be shipped to the delivery address provided by the customer. Please check your address and contact details carefully before confirming your order. Address changes may not be possible after dispatch.

4. Shipping Charges

Any applicable shipping or delivery charges will be shown during checkout or communicated before the order is confirmed. Refundability of shipping charges, where relevant, will be handled according to the applicable order terms and law.

5. Delivery Delays

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed as guaranteed. Delays may occur due to courier operations, weather, public holidays, regional restrictions, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control. If tracking information is available, customers should use it to check shipment progress.

6. Digital Products and Services

If an order is for a digital product or service rather than a physical item, delivery may take place electronically, such as through email, an account, or an online download. The applicable delivery method will be communicated when the product or service is offered.

7. Contact Us

For shipping or delivery questions, contact us at info@dantanisgroup.online and include your order details, where applicable.
